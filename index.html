My approach

Coaching that starts with your strengths

Coaching here is about helping you build on the strengths you already have, rather than focusing on what you think is missing. It is grounded in three ideas.

Strengths-based

We name what is already working for you, the qualities and habits that got you here, and put them to deliberate use in the challenges ahead.

Practical

Every conversation leads somewhere concrete. You leave each session with clearer thinking and a real step you can take in your work this week.

Future-focused

We spend less time on what went wrong and more on where you want to go, and on the leader you are growing into.

I create a safe and honest space where you can challenge unhelpful patterns, explore new perspectives and commit to meaningful action.
